What moves the price

The range is honest.
Here is what sets the number.

Most jobs land in the middle of the published range. Four things move the final price, and all four are explained before work starts.

Parts cost

A factory part sets the floor. The part price and the labor line are shown separately on the invoice.

Access and location

A built-in refrigerator or a unit in a tight cabinet takes longer than a freestanding one. The zone and parking do not add a trip fee.

Brand and age

Luxury and discontinued models can need a specialist part. If it is hard to source, you hear the price and ETA before we order.

After-hours work

Evenings and Sundays are scheduled at the same published range. Emergency commercial call-outs are quoted before dispatch.

How a price is held

One fee, one price,
before the work starts.

No parts are ordered and no work is billed until you approve the number on the invoice.

  1. Call or book online

    Tell us the appliance, the fault and the ZIP. We quote the diagnostic on the call and give you a two-hour arrival window.

  2. Diagnostic on site

    The technician tests the appliance, writes down what failed, and credits the $89 toward the repair if you proceed.

  3. Price approved

    You get the repair price and the parts list before anything is ordered or fitted. No work starts without your approval.

  4. Repair and warranty

    Most repairs are finished on the same visit. Parts and labor are covered for 90 days, written on the invoice.
