Common faults

What usually goes wrong
with a refrigerator.

Seven faults cover most call-outs. If yours is on the list, the part is usually already on the van.

  • Not cooling

    Compressor start components, evaporator fan or a defrost failure. Tested at temperature before we leave.

  • Not freezing

    Freezer holding at refrigerator temperature: defrost heater, thermistor or the air damper.

  • Water on the floor

    Blocked drain line, cracked defrost trough or a split door gasket. Floor dried and checked before we go.

  • Ice maker stopped

    Water inlet valve, filter or the ice-maker module. Line flushed and tested before we leave.

  • Frost in the freezer

    Defrost heater, thermistor or the timer. Frost across the back wall usually means the heater.

  • Running all the time

    Blocked condenser coils, a tired fan motor or a compressor losing pressure. Coils cleaned first.

  • Door not sealing

    Gaskets, hinges and levelling. A poor seal runs the compressor longer than it should.
