Who we are

A small crew,
a published rate card.

The same rule runs every visit: test the appliance, write down what failed, agree the price, then repair. No coupons, no dispatch fee, no surprise line items.

Fix Spark started with one rule from the trade: quote the job before you touch it. The diagnostic is $89 and is credited toward the repair. Every appliance we service has a published range, and the technician confirms the exact price on site before ordering a part or picking up a tool.

That rule shapes the day. Common parts ride on the van, so most jobs finish on the first visit. When a part has to be ordered, you get its price and ETA before we leave. When an appliance is beyond economical repair, we say so before you spend more on it.

Residential appliances and light commercial equipment go to the same licensed crew, on the same rate card, across four service zones. One number, one invoice, and a 90-day warranty on parts and labor written on the invoice.

How we work

Four rules
that do not change.

The appliance changes, the fault changes, the price changes. The sequence of the visit does not.

Price before the visit

The $89 diagnostic is published, and the repair range is on the page before you call. Nothing is ordered until you approve the number.

One technician, start to finish

The person who diagnoses the appliance is the person who repairs it. You hear the fault and the part in plain language, not a call-centre script.

Common parts on the van

Start components, fans, thermistors, valves and gaskets are stocked for the appliance types we cover. Rare parts are quoted with a delivery date.

Warranty in writing

Parts and labor are covered for 90 days. The warranty is printed on the invoice and applies to the repair we performed.
