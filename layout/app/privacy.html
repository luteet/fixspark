Questions about this policy?

Call (619) 555-0142 or email service@fixspark.com. We answer Monday – Saturday, 7 AM – 7 PM.

Fix Spark Appliance Repair repairs residential appliances and light commercial kitchen equipment across San Diego County. To schedule a visit, quote a repair and honor the warranty, we need a small set of details about you and the appliance. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it and the choices you have. It applies to fixspark.com and to requests handled by our dispatchers and technicians.

Information we collect

We ask only for details that are needed to schedule, price and complete a repair. You can call us without submitting the online form, but some information is required before a technician can be dispatched.

Information you give us

  • Contact details from the booking form: your name, phone number and ZIP code.
  • The appliance and service you select, and anything you tell us about the fault, its symptoms or the brand and model.
  • Service address, unit number, gate or access instructions when you provide them by phone, email or text message.
  • Photos, receipts or previous repair paperwork you choose to share with us.
  • Correspondence with our dispatchers and technicians, including call notes and messages about an appointment.

Payment and warranty records

After a visit we keep the invoice, the parts used, the labor performed and the payment status. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers on our own systems.

Information collected automatically

When you browse fixspark.com, our hosting and analytics tools may record basic technical data: the pages you viewed, the site or search that referred you, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not request precise GPS location.

How we use your information

We use personal information to run the service you asked for, and for the records a licensed repair business is expected to keep. Specifically:

  • To call you back, confirm the appliance and schedule a visit, usually within 15 minutes during business hours.
  • To diagnose the appliance, prepare a quote and agree the exact price before work starts.
  • To order parts, arrange a return visit and keep the appointment on schedule.
  • To invoice the repair, take payment and keep warranty records for the 90-day parts and labor coverage.
  • To answer questions, resolve complaints and follow up on a completed repair.
  • To keep records required by tax, licensing and insurance rules.
  • To understand which pages and services are useful, and to improve the site and the booking flow.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse of the booking form and abuse of our staff.

We do not sell your personal information. We send appointment reminders and service updates as part of the repair. Marketing messages are sent only when you have asked for them, and you can opt out at any time by replying to the message or calling us.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only where it is needed to complete a repair or to operate the business:

  • Our dispatchers and technicians, who need the address, appliance details and access notes to do the work.
  • Parts suppliers and warehouses, when a part has to be ordered for your appliance.
  • Payment processors and invoicing tools that handle payment for the repair.
  • Phone, text message, email and hosting providers that carry our communications and run the site.
  • Analytics providers that measure aggregate site traffic.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and authorities when the law requires disclosure or when we need to protect our legal rights.

Service providers receive only the information they need for their task, and they are not allowed to use it for their own marketing.

Cookies and site data

Fix Spark uses a small number of cookies and similar storage to keep the site working: remembering form state during a booking, supporting the page transition between sections, and measuring aggregate traffic. We do not run advertising cookies or sell audience data to ad networks.

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them may mean the booking form or some page features behave differently, but you can always call us instead.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as it serves the purpose it was collected for:

  • Booking requests that never become a visit: up to 12 months.
  • Service records, invoices and warranty documentation: at least four years, to cover warranty, tax and insurance obligations.
  • Call notes and messages about a completed visit: up to 24 months.
  • Marketing consent: until you withdraw it.

When a record is no longer needed, we delete it or remove the details that identify you.

Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete personal information, unless we must keep it for a legal or warranty reason.
  • Tell you the categories of information we collected and the categories of providers we shared it with.
  • Stop sending marketing messages.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, call (619) 555-0142 or email service@fixspark.com. We may ask for your phone number and service address to verify the request, and we aim to respond within 45 days. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with written permission.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information: access is limited to staff and providers who need it, service records sit on access-controlled systems, and card payments are handled by a payment processor rather than stored by us.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send card numbers, government identification or other sensitive information by ordinary email. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

Children's privacy

Fix Spark's services are intended for adults who can authorize work on a property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A minor should not submit a booking request without a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The revised version is posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will highlight it on the site. Continuing to use fixspark.com or requesting service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
