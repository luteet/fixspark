These terms describe how Fix Spark Appliance Repair handles a repair visit, from the first call to the invoice and the warranty that follows. They apply to residential appliances and light commercial kitchen equipment in San Diego County. Please read them before you authorize work; if anything is unclear, call us and we will explain it before a technician is dispatched.

When these terms apply

By submitting a booking request, calling us to schedule a visit or allowing a technician to begin work, you agree to these terms. They form the agreement between you and Fix Spark for the appliance described in the booking or invoice.

If you book on behalf of a household, a tenant, a business or a property you manage, you confirm that you are authorized to approve the visit and the repair. For commercial accounts, the person who places the request is treated as the authorized contact for that property.

These terms do not limit any right you have under California law that cannot be waived.

Diagnostic, estimates and approval

Every visit starts with a diagnostic. The diagnostic fee is $89 and is credited toward the repair if you approve the work. The fee covers the technician's time testing the appliance, identifying the fault and preparing the estimate.

Published repair ranges cover parts and labor: residential repairs typically fall between $99 and $420 depending on the appliance and the failed part, and commercial kitchen equipment starts at $189. The range is published before you call, but it is not the final price.

How the price is agreed

  • The technician confirms the exact price on site, before any part is ordered or any tool is used.
  • No work begins until you approve the number.
  • If a part or additional labor changes the price, you are told before we proceed.
  • If the appliance is beyond economical repair, we say so before you spend more on it.

The approved price covers the repair described on the invoice. It does not include building modifications, permits, work on other appliances or repairs made necessary by a separate fault discovered later.

Appointments and cancellations

We confirm appointments by phone and give a two-hour arrival window. During business hours we aim to call back within 15 minutes and, where the schedule allows, to offer a same-day visit when you call before noon.

  • Please provide accurate access details, including gate codes, parking notes and any pets we should know about.
  • Rescheduling or cancelling is free when you tell us at least two hours before the arrival window.
  • If nobody is available at the agreed time, we will try to reschedule. Repeated visits where access is not possible may require prepayment before the next appointment.
  • Technicians work Monday – Saturday, 7 AM – 7 PM. Requests outside those hours are arranged in advance.

Parts and return visits

The van carries the parts most visits need: start components, fans, thermistors, valves and gaskets for the appliance types we cover. When a part is not on the van, we quote its price and delivery date before we leave.

  • Special-order parts may require a deposit; the deposit is applied to the invoice when the repair is completed.
  • The return visit is scheduled at the same approved price unless the scope of work changes and you approve the difference.
  • If a part is unavailable or backordered, we will offer to wait, an alternative part or a cancellation before the part is ordered.
  • Parts removed from your appliance are not returned unless you ask for them before the visit.

Payment and invoices

Payment is due when the repair is completed. The invoice lists the diagnostic, the parts, the labor and the total, and it carries the warranty terms. We accept the payment methods shown on the invoice at the time of service.

Commercial customers can request invoicing per property when the account is set up in advance. Property managers should provide a purchase order number if their process requires one. Unpaid invoices may be subject to the collection terms stated on the invoice.

90-day parts and labor warranty

Fix Spark warrants the repair we performed for 90 days from the completion date, covering both the parts we supplied and the labor we carried out. The warranty is printed on the invoice and applies to the appliance and repair described there.

What the warranty covers

  • A part we supplied that fails within the warranty period under normal residential or commercial use.
  • An installation or workmanship fault in the repair we performed.
  • A return visit to correct a covered failure, scheduled at no additional labor charge.

What the warranty does not cover

  • Damage caused by misuse, neglect, power surges, flooding, pests or an unrelated fault.
  • Cosmetic damage, normal wear, or a new failure in a part we did not replace.
  • Work performed by another company or by the customer after our visit.
  • Appliance replacement or consequential costs such as lost food or lost business income.

A manufacturer's warranty on a part may last longer than 90 days. Where that applies, it passes to you and is handled under the manufacturer's terms.

Commercial work

Cafes, restaurants, offices, salons, short-term rentals and property managers receive the same licensed technicians and the same rate card as residential customers. Commercial kitchen equipment starts at $189 after the $89 diagnostic, and priority scheduling is available.

After-hours or emergency commercial service is quoted before dispatch. One invoice is issued per property unless a different arrangement is agreed in writing.

Limitation of liability

We repair the appliance described on the invoice and warrant that repair for 90 days. We do not guarantee the remaining life of the appliance or of parts we did not replace.

To the extent permitted by law, Fix Spark is not liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages, including lost food, lost business income, rental costs or damage to property that was not caused by our negligence. Our total liability for a repair is limited to the amount you paid for that repair. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded under California law.

Please remove personal items from the appliance and the surrounding area before the visit. We are not responsible for items left inside an appliance.

Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of California. If a problem arises, contact us first so we can put it right. Most concerns are resolved with a return visit under the warranty.

If a dispute cannot be resolved directly, it may be brought in the state or federal courts serving San Diego County, California. Either side may also use small claims court where the claim qualifies.
