Common Sub-Zero faults

Seven faults cover
most Sub-Zero call-outs.

If yours is on the list, the part is usually already on the van. The price is confirmed before anything is ordered.

  • Not cooling

    Compressor start components, evaporator fan or a defrost failure. Sealed-system pressures are logged before the quote.

  • Freezer not freezing

    Defrost heater, thermistor or the air damper. Tested at temperature before we leave.

  • Frost on the back wall

    Defrost heater or timer. Frost across the back wall usually means the heater.

  • Ice maker stopped

    Water inlet valve, filter or the ice-maker module. Line flushed and tested on the same visit.

  • Water on the floor

    Blocked drain line, cracked defrost trough or a split door gasket. Floor dried and checked before we go.

  • Running all the time

    Blocked condenser coils, a tired fan motor or a compressor losing pressure. Coils cleaned first.

  • Wine storage running warm

    Thermistor, evaporator fan or condenser airflow. Temperature held and logged before we leave.
